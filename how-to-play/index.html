THE RULES
  1. Start with one finger on the bottom-left square.
  2. Drag a single route to the flag in the top-right. Every square you cross is +1.
  3. Green squares add. Orange squares multiply. Red squares hurt.
  4. Never cross your own path.
  5. Bank at the flag. In Campaign, beat the star score to move on.

Strategy: how to get bigger scores

1. Build before you multiply. A multiplier is only as good as the number it multiplies. Collect greens and long runs of +1 squares first, then hit the orange squares late.

2. Snake, don't sprint. The shortest route to the flag is almost never the best one. Sweep back and forth in rows to cross as many squares as you can.

3. Keep an exit. Before you commit to a corner, check you can still get out without touching your own trail. Most bad runs end in a dead end you walked into ten moves earlier.

4. Price the red squares. Sometimes the only way to a big multiplier runs through a red square. Take the hit when the payoff is worth it.

5. Read the board first. Spend five seconds finding the multipliers and the red squares before your finger goes down. On the Daily board you only want one good run.

Modes

Daily: one board for everyone, every day, with a world ranking. Campaign: worlds of hand-built boards with star targets and lives. Zen: endless boards, no lives.
