Before you start
  • Find a quiet room with soft stuff around (a closet full of clothes is perfect). No fans or TV.
  • Hold the phone about a hand's width from your mouth, slightly off to the side.
  • Tap Guide to hear the pacing, then Record. Read it like you're telling a friend about a game you love.
  • Aim for the white line on the timer; never go past the red one. Re-record as often as you like; only the take you send is used.
