One finger. One route. One shot to bank the biggest number.

Drag from the bottom-left corner to the flag in the top-right. Every square you cross is +1. Green squares add, orange squares multiply, red squares hurt, and the order you take them changes everything. Snake through the whole board, but never cross your own path.

Campaign worlds, a Daily board shared by everyone with a live world ranking, and an endless Zen mode. Free on the App Store for iPhone.
