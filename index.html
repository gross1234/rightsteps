IN ONE SENTENCE

RightSteps is a free daily path puzzle for iPhone: drag one route from the bottom-left corner to the flag, stack your additions before your multipliers, and never cross your own path.

How it works

Draw one route

Start bottom-left, finish at the flag top-right. Every square you cross is +1.

Green adds

Grab them early so there is more for the multipliers to multiply.

Orange multiplies

The order you take them in changes everything.

Red hurts

Route around them, or take the hit if the payoff is worth it.

The one rule that makes it hard: you can never cross your own path. Full rules and strategy tips.

Three ways to play

Daily

One board for everyone, every day, with a live world ranking and your percentile.

Campaign

Worlds of hand-built boards. Beat the star score to move on.

Zen

Endless boards. No lives, no timer, no pressure.

Questions

Is RightSteps free?

Yes. Optional purchases remove ads, add hints on every level, or give unlimited Campaign lives.

Is there a daily puzzle?

Yes. Everyone plays the same Daily board, and once your score syncs through Game Center you see the world's best and your percentile.

How do I lose a life?

Only in Campaign, and only when you bank at the flag below the first-star score. Lives come back one every 15 minutes. Daily and Zen never use lives.

Is it on Android?

iPhone now. Android is in a closed beta on Google Play.

Do I need an account?

No. Progress stays on your device. See the privacy policy.
