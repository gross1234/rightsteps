Privacy Policy

HomeSupportPrivacy

Effective October 7, 2026

RightSteps ("the app") is developed by Gabriel Ross ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why. Short version: the game collects no personal information, keeps your progress on your device, and sends only anonymous gameplay statistics (no name, email, phone or advertising ID) so we can improve the game. It keeps your progress on your device. Advertising and Game Center are provided by Google and Apple under their own policies.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell personal information. There is no account, no sign-up and no email list. Your levels, stars, coins, settings and purchase state are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Anonymous gameplay statistics

To see which levels are too hard, whether the daily puzzle is fun and whether ads and offers are working, the app sends us anonymous usage events: app opens and session length, levels and daily puzzles played (score, stars, steps), screens such as the subscription page being shown, purchases and free trials started (product and price, never payment details), and ad impressions with the ad revenue estimate Google reports. Each event carries a random ID created on your device (not your Apple ID, advertising identifier or device name), the app and iOS version, and your language/region setting.

These statistics are not linked to your identity, are not combined with data from other companies, are not used for tracking or targeted advertising, and are never sold. They are stored with our database provider (Supabase, in the United States) and deleted after about 400 days. Deleting the app removes the random ID; reinstalling creates a new one.

Third-party services

Google AdMob (advertising). The free version shows ads served by Google. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (if you allow tracking), IP address, coarse location derived from IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data. We do not receive this data in identifiable form. See Google's advertising policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can limit personalized ads in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and buying "Remove Ads Forever" or "Pro" turns ads off entirely.

Apple Game Center (leaderboards). If you are signed in to Game Center and choose to use leaderboards, your scores and Game Center nickname are submitted to Apple and shown to other players under Apple's privacy policy. You can sign out of Game Center in iOS Settings at any time.

Apple In-App Purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. Receipts are validated on the device.

Local notifications. Reminders (lives refilled, new daily board, streak at risk) are scheduled on your device and never leave it. Turn them off in iOS Settings → Notifications.

Children

RightSteps is rated for ages 4+ but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are requested with Google's child-directed treatment disabled; parents can disable personalized ads through iOS tracking settings.

Your choices

  • Ads: remove them permanently with the "Remove Ads Forever" or "Pro" purchase, or limit tracking in iOS Settings.
  • Progress: Settings → Reset progress inside the app, or delete the app.
  • Game Center: sign out in iOS Settings.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for material changes, note it in the app's release notes.
