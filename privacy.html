Effective October 7, 2026

RightSteps ("the app") is developed by Gabriel Ross ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why. Short version: there is no account and we never ask for your name. The app uses a random install ID for anonymous gameplay statistics and to connect purchases you make on our checkout page to your copy of the game. Payments are handled by Stripe; advertising and Game Center are provided by Google and Apple under their own policies.

Information we collect

No account, no sign-up, nothing sold. Your levels, stars, coins and settings are stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Install ID. On first launch the app creates a random identifier (not your device's advertising identifier, and not linked to your name, email or Apple ID). It is used for the two purposes below and nothing else.

Anonymous gameplay statistics. The app sends gameplay events (for example level started, level completed, ad shown, purchase screen opened) with the install ID, app version, iOS version and language to our analytics database. We use them only to improve the game. They are never used for advertising or shared with anyone.

Purchases. When you buy something on the RightSteps checkout page, we store the install ID, what you bought, the amount, the date, your Stripe customer and subscription IDs, subscription status and renewal date, and the email address you entered at checkout. We use this to unlock your purchase in the app, show your Premium status, let you cancel or restore, and keep business and tax records.

Third-party services

Stripe (payments). Purchases are paid on our web checkout page, sold by Sort Inc. (Convivial Concierge) and processed by Stripe. Your card or bank details go directly to Stripe; we never see or store them. Stripe also sends your receipts.

Supabase (hosting). Our gameplay statistics and purchase records are stored with Supabase in the United States.

Google AdMob (advertising). The free version shows ads served by Google. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (if you allow tracking), IP address, coarse location derived from IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data. We do not receive this data in identifiable form. See Google's advertising policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can limit personalized ads in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Premium, "Remove Ads Forever" or "Pro" turns ads off entirely.

Apple Game Center (leaderboards). If you are signed in to Game Center and choose to use leaderboards, your scores and Game Center nickname are submitted to Apple and shown to other players under Apple's privacy policy. You can sign out of Game Center in iOS Settings at any time.

Local notifications. Reminders (lives refilled, new daily board, streak at risk) are scheduled on your device and never leave it. Turn them off in iOS Settings → Notifications.

Retention

Gameplay statistics are deleted after 13 months. Purchase records are kept as long as needed to provide what you bought and to meet tax and accounting obligations.

Children

RightSteps is rated for ages 4+ but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Purchases require an adult's payment card. Ads are requested with Google's child-directed treatment disabled; parents can disable personalized ads through iOS tracking settings.

Your choices

  • Ads: remove them with Premium, "Remove Ads Forever" or "Pro", or limit tracking in iOS Settings.
  • Subscription: cancel any time in the app under Profile → Premium.
  • Your purchase data: email us to get a copy or have it deleted (we may need to keep payment records the law requires).
  • Progress: Settings → Reset progress inside the app, or delete the app.
  • Game Center: sign out in iOS Settings.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for material changes, note it in the app's release notes.
