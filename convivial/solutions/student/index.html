Resident concierge for purpose-built student housing

The short answer

Convivial Concierge runs move-in day operations, high-volume package handling, community events and parent communications for purpose-built student housing, so the leasing team can focus on pre-leasing for next year.

What Convivial Concierge runs at student housing properties

Move-in day operations

Scheduling, welcome and hosting for the busiest weekend of the year.

Package room management

High-volume intake and pickup that does not swamp the front desk.

Community events

Events that build a community and drive word-of-mouth pre-leasing.

Parent communications

A trusted contact that parents can reach.

Front-desk hospitality

Staffed or virtual coverage for extended hours.

What the owner gets

  • Smoother move-in
  • Word-of-mouth that helps pre-leasing
  • Leasing staff freed from the package room
  • Reporting by semester

Frequently asked questions

What does concierge do in student housing?

Concierge runs the front desk, packages, move-in logistics and community events, and is a point of contact for parents.

Does concierge help student housing pre-leasing?

Residents who enjoy the community renew and refer friends, and a well-run move-in is a strong first impression.

Can you staff just move-in season?

Yes. Coverage can be scoped seasonally or year-round.

Do you work alongside our property manager?

Yes. Concierge complements the on-site management team.
