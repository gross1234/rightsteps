Concierge programs by property type

The short answer

Convivial Concierge scopes concierge differently for each property type. Choose yours to see what we run, what the owner gets and common questions.

Multifamily & luxury residential

Convivial Concierge runs resident concierge for apartment, condo and build-to-rent communities: front-desk hospitality, package and errand handling, move-in welcomes, resident events and on-demand requests.

Mixed-use districts & master-planned communities

Convivial Concierge gives a mixed-use property one hospitality team that serves residents, office tenants, shoppers and visitors at once.

Hotels, resorts & branded residences

Convivial Concierge staffs, extends or fully runs the concierge desk for hotels, resorts and branded residences.

Office & tenant experience

Convivial Concierge runs lobby hospitality, visitor welcome, amenity-floor hosting, tenant events and workplace requests for office buildings.

Senior living

Convivial Concierge provides non-clinical lifestyle concierge for senior living and active-adult communities: errands, transportation booking, outings, events and coordination with residents' families.

Student housing

Convivial Concierge runs move-in day operations, high-volume package handling, community events and parent communications for purpose-built student housing, so the leasing team can focus on pre-leasing for next year.

Life science, medical office & healthcare

Convivial Concierge provides lobby hosting, patient and visitor wayfinding, amenity programming and tenant services for life science campuses and medical office buildings, so the people arriving find the building calm and easy to use.

Retail centres & lifestyle destinations

Convivial Concierge runs guest services for retail centres and lifestyle destinations: a staffed guest-services desk, hands-free shopping, events, visitor and tourism programs and retailer support, so shoppers stay longer and come back.
