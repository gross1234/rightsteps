Lifestyle concierge for senior living and active-adult communities

The short answer

Convivial Concierge provides non-clinical lifestyle concierge for senior living and active-adult communities: errands, transportation booking, outings, events and coordination with residents' families. Care stays with your operator; we make daily life feel more like a good hotel.

What Convivial Concierge runs at senior living properties

Lifestyle concierge

Outings and events

Calendars of trips, classes and social events that residents look forward to.

Family coordination

A point of contact for families arranging visits, deliveries and celebrations.

Rides and drivers arranged and confirmed.

Move-in welcome

A gentler first month for new residents and their families.

Frequently asked questions

Is concierge in senior living a clinical service?

No. Convivial's concierge is non-clinical lifestyle and hospitality service. Care and medical services stay with your licensed operator.

What does a senior living concierge do?

They handle errands, appointments, transportation booking, outings and events, and they act as a point of contact for families.

Does concierge help senior living occupancy?

It gives sales teams a tangible hospitality story on tours and keeps residents and families happier, which supports referrals.

Can you work alongside our operator?

Yes. Concierge coordinates with your operator's staff and does not replace them.
