A concierge program for Sanders Capital Partners / Tusk Asset Management's properties

You hold neighborhood retail; medical/behavioral health; light industrial across Southeast US; Midwest US; Southwest US. Here is how Convivial Concierge would bring one hospitality standard to those buildings, alongside your property managers.

At your life science and medical office properties

  • Visitor and patient welcome: Greeting, wayfinding and escort for first-time visitors.
  • Campus amenity programming: Events and hosted spaces that help life science tenants recruit and retain staff.
  • Tenant services: Reservations, deliveries and errands for tenant teams.

The full life science and medical office program

At your retail properties

  • Guest services desk: Information, gift cards, lost and found, stroller and wheelchair loans.
  • Hands-free shopping and carry-out: Purchases held and delivered to the car, home or hotel.
  • Events and activations: Seasonal programming that gives people a reason to visit.

The full retail program

We have left out your industrial holdings, where concierge is not usually the right fit.

A proposed first step

  1. Choose your flagship life science and medical office property. We walk it and scope hours, staffing and programs.
  2. 90-day pilot. Measures agreed up front, reported monthly to you and the property manager.
  3. Portfolio standard. If it works, the same standard rolls out to the rest of the portfolio, with one report.
