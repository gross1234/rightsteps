A concierge program for T5 Equity (Ghermezian Family)'s properties

You hold retail, mixed-use, entertainment across National + Canada. Here is how Convivial Concierge would bring one hospitality standard to those buildings, alongside your property managers.

At your mixed-use properties

  • One team across every use: The same hosts serve the residential lobby, the office lobby and the plaza, so service is consistent everywhere.
  • District events and programming: Markets, seasonal events and tenant mixers that bring foot traffic to your retail.
  • Retail and restaurant partnerships: Resident and tenant perks with your own retailers, so spending stays on site.

The full mixed-use program

At your retail properties

  • Guest services desk: Information, gift cards, lost and found, stroller and wheelchair loans.
  • Hands-free shopping and carry-out: Purchases held and delivered to the car, home or hotel.
  • Events and activations: Seasonal programming that gives people a reason to visit.

The full retail program

A proposed first step

  1. Choose your flagship mixed-use property. We walk it and scope hours, staffing and programs.
  2. 90-day pilot. Measures agreed up front, reported monthly to you and the property manager.
  3. Portfolio standard. If it works, the same standard rolls out to the rest of the portfolio, with one report.
