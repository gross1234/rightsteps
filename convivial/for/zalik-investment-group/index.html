A concierge program for Zalik Investment Group's properties

You hold class a office (distressed/value-add) across Atlanta metro (Sandy Springs, Central Perimeter, Midtown, Alpharetta). Here is how Convivial Concierge would bring one hospitality standard to those buildings, alongside your property managers.

At your office properties

  • Lobby hospitality and visitor welcome: A host instead of a security desk: guests are greeted, registered and walked up.
  • Amenity floor hosting: Staffing for lounges, conference centres, fitness and rooftop spaces so they actually get used.
  • Tenant events and programming: Regular events that make the building a community and bring people back to the office.

A proposed first step

  2. 90-day pilot. Measures agreed up front, reported monthly to you and the property manager.
