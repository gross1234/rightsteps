Concierge at Eight Winds

Luxury multifamily, 280 units, St. Augustine, FL. Acquired February 2025.

Front-desk hospitality

Hosts at the lobby desk, or a virtual concierge for smaller buildings, who greet residents and guests by name.

Packages, deliveries and errands

Package intake, dry cleaning, grocery and pet-care coordination handled before it reaches the leasing office.

Move-in and renewal moments

Welcome arrivals, neighbourhood guides and small touches timed around the renewal window.

Resident events and community

A monthly calendar that gets neighbours to know each other, which is what makes people stay.

On-demand requests

Dinner reservations, home services and travel help booked through one trusted contact.

What DeBartolo Holdings (DeBartolo Development) gets as the owner

  • A stronger renewal conversation
  • An amenity story that supports premium rents
  • Fewer non-maintenance requests landing on the leasing team
  • Monthly reporting on requests, response times and resident sentiment

Pilot at Eight Winds

A 90-day pilot with measures agreed up front: requests handled, response time, event attendance and residents satisfaction. Reported monthly to DeBartolo Holdings (DeBartolo Development) and the property manager.
