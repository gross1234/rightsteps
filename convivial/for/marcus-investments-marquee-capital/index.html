A concierge program for Marcus Investments / Marquee Capital's properties

You hold senior housing, retail (neighborhood and community centers) across Midwest primary; national secondary. Here is how Convivial Concierge would bring one hospitality standard to those buildings, alongside your property managers.

At your senior living properties

  • Lifestyle concierge: Appointments, reservations, gifts and errands, handled by a familiar face.
  • Outings and events: Calendars of trips, classes and social events that residents look forward to.
  • Family coordination: A point of contact for families arranging visits, deliveries and celebrations.

The full senior living program

At your retail properties

  • Guest services desk: Information, gift cards, lost and found, stroller and wheelchair loans.
  • Hands-free shopping and carry-out: Purchases held and delivered to the car, home or hotel.
  • Events and activations: Seasonal programming that gives people a reason to visit.

The full retail program

A proposed first step

  1. Choose your flagship senior living property. We walk it and scope hours, staffing and programs.
  2. 90-day pilot. Measures agreed up front, reported monthly to you and the property manager.
  3. Portfolio standard. If it works, the same standard rolls out to the rest of the portfolio, with one report.
