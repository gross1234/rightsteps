Common questions

How do I lose a life?
Only in Campaign, and only when you bank at the flag below the first-star score. Lives come back one every 15 minutes. Daily and Zen never use lives. Pro has unlimited lives.

I bought Pro or Remove Ads on another device.
Open the shop (tap the coin pill on the home screen) and tap Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID.

Ads are still showing after I paid.
Force-quit the app and reopen it, then use Restore purchases. If it persists, email us with your App Store receipt.

Where is my Game Center rank?
Sign in to Game Center in iOS Settings. The Daily card shows the world's best score and your percentile once your score syncs.

How do I delete my data?
All progress is stored on your device. Settings → Reset progress wipes it, and deleting the app removes everything. See the privacy policy.
